Autochrome is a private daily journal for one warm moment at a time. You save today with a feeling, and you can attach a photo or a short voice clip, then keep that day with the others. It is for someone who wants matching days to stand out on this device, not a social feed.

• Save one warm moment for today with a feeling
• Feeling names Amber, Harbor, Linen and Grove, which you can rename in Settings
• Attach photo or Add voice on the Save a moment form
• Keep this day so it joins the other days
• On Moments, a glass row of kept days; only days that share today's feeling stand out
• Open a matching day from the glass row or Open {feeling}
• Garden lists every kept day, newest first; Open this moment to read a Moment
• Show introduction again without erasing
• Erase saved moments on this device
• Contact Autochrome in Settings
• Saved moments, photos and voice clips stay on this device

After the introduction, Moments is the main screen. Portrait only, on iPhone and iPad. Light appearance only. Requires the full screen on iPad. English copy only.
