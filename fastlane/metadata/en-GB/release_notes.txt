This is the first release of Autochrome, a private daily journal for one warm moment at a time.

• Save today with a feeling on the Save a moment form
• Feeling names Amber, Harbor, Linen and Grove, which you can rename in Settings
• Optional photo via Attach photo
• Optional short voice clip via Add voice
• Keep this day so it joins the other days
• Glass row of kept days; only days that share today's feeling stand out
• Open a matching day from the glass row or Open {feeling}
• Garden list of every kept day, newest first
• Read a saved Moment from Garden
• Show introduction again without erasing
• Erase saved moments on this device
• Contact Autochrome in Settings

Saved moments, photos and voice clips stay on this device. Portrait only, on iPhone and iPad. Light appearance only. English copy only.
