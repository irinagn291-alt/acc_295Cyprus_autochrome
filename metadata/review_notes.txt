The only permission is the microphone, requested after Add voice on the Save a moment form, then Continue on "A short voice clip stays on this device." Continue is the only in-app control before the system microphone dialog. The usage description is "A short voice clip stays on this device with the moment you save." If the microphone is off, the form shows "Voice stays off. Open Settings if you want a clip later." and Open Settings (device Settings). Attach photo opens the system photo picker. The app does not show its own photo-permission screen and has no photo usage string. There are no camera, photos-library, location, or tracking permission strings.

There is no login, no in-app purchase, no public or shared feed of user-generated content, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Autochrome is a private daily journal for one warm moment at a time. You save today with a feeling, and you can attach a photo or a short voice clip, then keep that day with the others. It is for someone who wants matching days to stand out on this device, not a social feed.

No credentials or demo account are needed. Cold launch shows a system launch screen with no words, then a brief empty screen. If the introduction is not finished, three pages appear. Each page has an illustration with no words, a title, a line of body copy, page dots, a primary button, and Skip. 1. Good days, kept close — "Save today's warm moment with a feeling, a photo, or a short voice clip." Next. 2. Days you can look back on — "Keeping a day puts it with the others." Next. 3. Open a matching day — "Only days that share today's feeling stand out. Tap one to open it." Continue. Continue on the last page, or Skip on any page, ends the introduction and opens Moments. If the introduction was already finished, launch goes straight to Moments. On the Simulator only, the introduction is skipped and four kept days are created once (Harbor, Linen, Grove, Amber). Moments opens on Open, "Today is Amber. Tap that day to open it," and Open Amber. To reach empty Moments, open Settings, tap Erase saved moments, and confirm with Erase moments on this device, then Skip or Continue. Empty Moments shows Nothing saved yet; the first Save this moment only opens the Save a moment form. Save by tapping Save this moment on the form (Amber starts selected among Amber, Harbor, Linen, Grove). After Add voice, Continue is shown before the system microphone dialog. Stop voice finishes the clip. You can save without a clip. Garden and Settings are only on Moments once at least one moment has been saved. Keep this day appears when today is saved and no kept day shares that feeling. Open {feeling} or a matching glass day stays on Moments and shows "{feeling} is open again." Garden (Close) lists Past moments, newest first. Open this moment opens Moment. If a photo or voice clip was saved, Moment states that; the photo is not shown and there is no play control. Settings has Emotion labels, Show introduction again, Erase saved moments, and Contact Autochrome.

Saved moments, photos, and voice clips stay on this device. Support is Contact Autochrome in Settings.
